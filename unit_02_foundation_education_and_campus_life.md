## 📖 PHẦN 1.2: LÝ THUYẾT NGỮ PHÁP 2 TẦNG (2-TIER GRAMMAR THEORY)

### 🔵 TẦNG 1: NGỮ PHÁP CƠ BẢN (BAND 3.5 - 4.0 FOUNDATION)
1. **Thì Quá khứ đơn (Past Simple):**
   - Hành động đã chấm dứt hoàn toàn trong quá khứ, có thời gian xác định (*yesterday, last week, in 2024, ago*).
   - Công thức: `(+) S + V2/ed` | `(-) S + did not + V-bare` | `(?) Did + S + V-bare?`
   - *Ví dụ:* "Yesterday, Nam **completed** his research assignment."
2. **Thì Hiện tại hoàn thành (Present Perfect):**
   - Hành động xảy ra trong quá khứ nhưng kết quả vẫn còn ở hiện tại, hoặc kể trải nghiệm (*since, for, already, ever*).
   - Công thức: `(+) S + have/has + V3/ed` | `(-) S + have/has not + V3/ed` | `(?) Have/Has + S + V3/ed?`
   - *Ví dụ:* "He **has studied** at this institution for three months."
3. **Mạo từ A / An / The:**
   - `A / An`: Danh từ đếm được số ít nhắc tới lần đầu (*a lecture, an assignment*).
   - `The`: Danh từ xác định cả hai bên đều biết rõ (*the library, the deadline of this project*).

### 🟣 TẦNG 2: NGỮ PHÁP NÂNG CAO (BAND 4.5 - 5.0+ ACADEMIC UPGRADE)
1. **Câu phức với liên từ phụ thuộc (Complex Sentences):**
   - *Nguyên nhân (Because):* "Because the curriculum is challenging, students must maintain self-discipline."
   - *Nhượng bộ (Although):* "Although the assignment was difficult, we submitted it before the deadline."
   - *Hành động song song (While):* "While the tutor was explaining, students were taking notes actively."
2. **Bẫy mạo từ học thuật:**
   - ❌ *The students should collaborate...* (Sai nếu nói chung chung).
   - ✅ *Students should collaborate with peers to develop their potential.* (Đúng khi nói chung).

## 📰 PHẦN 5: BÀI ĐỌC HỌC THUẬT (READING PASSAGE — CHUẨN BAND 3.5)
**Tiêu đề:** *Studying at University: What New Students Need to Know* (~260 từ)

**[Paragraph A - Campus & Curriculum]**  
Every year, many young adults enter higher education **institutions** to start their undergraduate studies. In the first semester, students learn a new **academic** **curriculum** with many different subjects. Instead of just listening to theoretical **lectures** in large halls, learners now have opportunities to **collaborate** with peers on group projects. This modern method helps students **absorb** knowledge faster and build confidence.

**[Paragraph B - Study Habits & Deadlines]**  
However, university life also requires strong personal **discipline**. Undergraduates often receive difficult **assignments** with strict **deadlines**. To **concentrate** well on their studies, learners should establish a quiet daily **revision** routine in the library. If students have questions about their lessons, they can always ask their course **tutor** for support.

**[Paragraph C - Evaluating Potential]**  
At the end of each semester, teachers **evaluate** student progress through tests and practical presentations. When students develop good learning habits, they can unlock their full **potential** and **qualify** for great future jobs.

## ✍️ PHẦN 6: ĐỀ BÀI VÀ BÀI VIẾT MẪU (WRITING LAB — CHUẨN BAND 3.5 SCAFFOLDING)
* **Đề bài:** *"Do you think university students should focus on studying theories or learning practical skills?"* (Viết 1 đoạn văn 60 - 90 từ).
* **Bài viết mẫu Band 4.5 - 5.0 vừa sức (kèm Audio giọng đọc bản xứ):**
  > "In my opinion, universities should teach both academic theory and practical skills. First, students need a strong curriculum to absorb foundational knowledge. Second, practical assignments help undergraduates collaborate with peers and prepare for real jobs. Therefore, balancing both methods is the best way for learners to qualify for their future careers."

## 🗣️ PHẦN 7: BỘ CÂU HỎI LUYỆN NÓI (SPEAKING LAB — CHUẨN BAND 3.5 MODEL SCAFFOLDING)

   💡 *Gợi ý mẫu:* "I prefer studying with friends because we can collaborate on difficult assignments. It is also more fun than studying alone."
2. **Question 2:** *What subject do you like most in your curriculum?*  
   💡 *Gợi ý mẫu:* "I enjoy English the most in my academic curriculum. It gives me a great opportunity to improve my speaking and absorb new ideas."
3. **Question 3:** *How do you usually concentrate when you have an important assignment?*  
   💡 *Gợi ý mẫu:* "I usually go to a quiet library to concentrate on my lessons. I also turn off my smartphone so I can meet the deadline easily."
